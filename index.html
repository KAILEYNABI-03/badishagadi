About us

Badishagadi Projects (Pty) Ltd supplies government departments, municipalities, schools and universities, hospitals and clinics, construction and property companies, manufacturers, mines, NGOs and private businesses.

Our aim is to be one of South Africa's most trusted suppliers of consumables, equipment and procurement solutions across the public and private sectors.

We keep to health, safety and environmental standards under South African law when we handle, store and deliver products, including hazardous goods and protective equipment.
